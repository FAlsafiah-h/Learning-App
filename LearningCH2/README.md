#  <#Learning App#>

This app is called LJ App.  It's used to help any learner stay consistant with achieving their goal. 

### Main Features: 
- Custom Goal Onboarding: Allows you to enter any learning topic of your choice and select a structured timeframe—Week, Month, or Year.
- Interactive Activity Dashboard: Features a horizontally scrollable date selector where you can easily toggle your daily status as Learned, Freezed, or Empty.
- Dynamic Freeze System: Automatically scales your available freeze limits according to your timeframe (2 freezes for a week, 8 for a month, and 96 for a year).
- Multi-Month Calendar View: A dedicated calendar screen that maps out your year month-by-month, using exact date storage keys to keep your history clean and isolated.
- Goal Completion & Status Screens: Automatically concludes your learning period when finished, showcasing customized success or non-completion screens.
